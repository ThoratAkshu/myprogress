How to run the master account
  1. Open a company current account. Keep creator money in a bank account in the business's name, separate from personal money, and add its details above.
  2. Add money, then record it. Transfer money into that account by NEFT, IMPS or UPI, then press Add money with the UTR so the balance here matches your bank.
  3. Add a payout provider when volume grows. RazorpayX and similar services let you top up a payout balance from this account and pay creators automatically on payment day.
  4. Pay tax that's held back. If you deduct tax from creator payments, pay it to the government and record it with Pay tax and the challan number. Ask a chartered accountant what applies to your business.
  5. Reconcile monthly. Compare this ledger with the bank statement and record anything missing.
