Setting up the master account

  1. Open a company current account. Keep creator money in a bank account in the business's name, separate from personal money. This is the master account.
  2. Add a payout provider when volume grows. RazorpayX, Cashfree Payouts, and similar services let you top up a payout balance from that account and send money to UPI IDs in bulk. Add the keys to the server settings and payment day sends everything for you.
  3. Until then, pay by hand. Send each withdrawal from net banking or UPI, then mark it paid here with the UTR.
  4. Check tax rules before going live. Paying creators can mean collecting PAN and deducting tax at source. Set the rate under Earning rules, pay what's held back to the government, and record it here as "Tax paid to government". Ask a chartered accountant what applies to your business.
  5. Reconcile monthly. The balance here should match the bank statement for the money you set aside.
